🚖 Taptix Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Version 2.2

1. Overview

Taptix is a driver-assistance app that helps ride-hailing and delivery drivers auto-accept requests hands-free. Privacy is built in — we do not operate any servers, do not collect analytics, and do not share any data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Location Data: Used entirely on-device for Map Zone geofencing. Never transmitted to any server.
  • Screen Content (Accessibility): Read locally to detect ride request buttons. No screen content is stored, logged, or transmitted outside your device.
  • Settings & Preferences: Stored locally using Android SharedPreferences on your device.
  • Activity Logs: App name, action type (Accepted/Rejected), and timestamp — stored only on your device and auto-deleted after 7 days.

3. What We Never Collect

  • Passenger names, phone numbers, or addresses
  • Exact fare amounts in activity logs
  • Screenshots, screen recordings, or photos
  • Contact list, call logs, or SMS
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID)
  • Browsing history or app usage outside Taptix

4. Network Access

Taptix connects to the internet only to check for app updates from GitHub Releases. All connections use HTTPS. No personal data is included in update requests.

5. Permissions Explained

  • Accessibility Service: Required to read screen content and simulate taps for auto-accepting ride requests. This is the core feature of Taptix.
  • Display Over Other Apps: Required for the floating control toolbar that overlays ride-hailing apps.
  • Location (Fine & Coarse): Required for Map Zone features. Used only locally to check if you are within your configured work area.
  • Microphone (Record Audio): Used only when Voice Commands feature is enabled. You can disable this at any time.
  • Vibration: For haptic feedback when rides are accepted. Can be disabled in Settings.
  • Internet: Only for checking app updates from GitHub.
  • Notifications: To show the persistent "Taptix is active" notification required for foreground services.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: To prevent Android from killing background services while you're driving.

6. Accessibility Service Disclosure

Taptix uses Android's Accessibility Service to scan screen content and simulate button taps on ride-hailing apps such as Uber Driver, Ola Driver, Rapido Captain, inDrive, Namma Yatri, and Porter. This data is processed exclusively on your device and is never stored persistently or transmitted.

7. Third-Party Services

Taptix does not integrate any third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase, Crashlytics), advertising networks, or tracking libraries.

8. Data Retention

  • Activity logs: auto-deleted after 7 days, capped at 150 entries
  • Settings: retained until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Location: never persisted beyond the current session check

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

10. Children's Privacy

Taptix is designed for professional drivers (18+) and is not intended for use by minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be included in app update release notes on GitHub.
