Taptix Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Important Summary: Taptix is a driver safety and ergonomics utility designed for commercial ride-hailing drivers. We do not collect, sell, store, or transmit any personal data, audio, passwords, credentials, or financial information. All processing occurs strictly on your local device.

1. Android Accessibility Service API Prominent Disclosure

Taptix requires the use of the Android AccessibilityService API to provide its core automated assistant functionality:

  • Purpose of Use: The Accessibility Service is used solely to dispatch automated touch gestures and screen taps on designated driver dispatch screens (e.g., accepting incoming ride trips). This allows drivers to interact safely with vehicle-mounted phones with minimal eye-off-road distraction.
  • No Keystroke or Input Logging: Taptix does NOT read, capture, or store text entered on virtual keyboards, passwords, credit card numbers, or messages.
  • Auto-Pause Protection: Taptix automatically pauses all gesture actions whenever the on-screen soft keyboard is visible to ensure privacy and prevent accidental inputs.
  • User Consent: The Accessibility Service is disabled by default and can only be activated with explicit, affirmative consent by the user via Android System Settings. It can be revoked at any time.

2. Display Over Other Apps (System Alert Window)

Taptix requests permission to draw over other applications. This is used exclusively to display a floating heads-up control widget that stays visible while running partner driver and navigation apps. No overlay content obscures system safety dialogues.

3. Data Collection and Transmission

Taptix operates under a strict zero-data-collection architecture:

  • Zero Remote Servers: We do not operate remote user tracking databases or sell data to third-party ad networks.
  • Local Storage: Your preferences (e.g., target apps, click frequency, night-mode intensity) are stored 100% locally on your device via encrypted Android SharedPreferences.
  • Updates: The application may verify GitHub release endpoints solely to inform you of newer versions. No device identifiers or telemetries are shared during update checks.

4. Children’s Privacy

Taptix is exclusively designed for licensed commercial adult drivers and does not address or knowingly interact with individuals under the age of 18.
